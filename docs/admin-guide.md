# Admin Guide

This guide is for healthcare center administrators using MediMind Portal.

## Getting Started

1. Launch MediMind Portal on your desktop.
2. Select the **Admin** tab on the login screen.
3. Enter your email and password. If 2FA is enabled, approve the prompt on your phone.
4. You will be taken to the **Admin Dashboard**.

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows today's KPIs: appointments, queue size, revenue, new patients. Charts update every 60 seconds.

## Queue Management

- **Live Queue** (`Queue` in sidebar): shows today's waiting patients in order.
- Click **Call Next** (or press **Space**) to advance the queue.
- Click **Add Walk-In** (or press **Ctrl+N**) to add a patient without an appointment.
- Click a queue entry to view patient details or reassign to a different doctor.

## Appointments

- **Pending**: new appointment requests awaiting approval. Click **Approve** or **Reject**.
- **Calendar**: month/week/day view of all appointments.
- **All Appointments**: filterable table — search by patient name, doctor, date, status.

## Doctors

The **Roster** shows all doctors at your center. Click a doctor to view their profile, schedule availability, and appointment history. Use **Add Doctor** to invite a new doctor to your center.

## Patients

The **Directory** is a searchable list of all patients who have visited your center. Click a patient to view their full visit history and active prescriptions.

## Analytics

| Sub-page | Contents |
|----------|----------|
| Dashboard | Revenue, appointment volume, top doctors |
| Revenue | Monthly/weekly revenue chart, payment method breakdown |
| Per Doctor | Per-doctor appointment count, completion rate, revenue |
| Export | Download reports in CSV or XLSX |

## Payments

The **Ledger** shows all payment transactions. Filter by date range, payment method, or status.

## Audit Log

Every admin action is logged with timestamp, actor, and details. Use filters to narrow by date, actor, or action type. Export to CSV for compliance records.

## Center Settings

| Page | What you can change |
|------|---------------------|
| General | Center name, phone, email, address, GPS coordinates, specializations |
| Branding | Logo, cover image, description |
| Working Hours | Open/close times per day, break periods |
| Booking Rules | Slot duration, advance booking window, cancellation policy, auto-approval, reminders |

## Inactivity Logout

The portal automatically logs you out after **30 minutes** of inactivity. You will see a notification toast when this happens. Log back in to continue.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

See the [Keyboard Shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md) reference, or press **Ctrl+/** inside the app.
